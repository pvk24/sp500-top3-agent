How the ranking works
  • Universe: every current S&P 500 member, fetched from Wikipedia's list of S&P 500 companies on each run. Alphabet is counted once, as GOOG (Class C). Stocks with under a year of trading history are skipped because the checks need 200 days of prices.
  • Your seven checks:
    • 200-DMA: price above its 200-day average.
    • Earnings: no earnings report in the next 14 days.
    • RSI: 14-day RSI between 30 and 65, or below 30 and rising (the bounce has started).
    • Momentum: positive 3-month return, or a positive 12-month trend with price back above its 20-day average.
    • P/E: forward P/E above 0 (profitable) and at most 40.
    • PEG: forward P/E divided by expected EPS growth to next year (in %) is at most 2.0. Growth is measured from this year's EPS, or the last 12 months' if this year's is negative. If neither is positive, PEG can't be computed and the check fails.
    • Sharpe ratio: 1-year Sharpe of at least 1.0, meaning annualised return above a 4% risk-free rate divided by annualised volatility, over the last 252 trading days.
  • Stage 1, all stocks: six checks run on all ~500 stocks (everything except earnings). Stocks that pass all six and aren't overbought or still falling go to the shortlist, up to 15.
  • Stage 2, shortlist: adds the earnings check and analyst data (target price, EPS estimate revisions, ratings), which are too slow to pull for every stock daily.
  • Score (0–100): 30% trend vs the 200-DMA, 30% momentum (3-month and 12-month), 15% entry timing (cooler RSI and discount to the 52-week high), 15% valuation (lower P/E and PEG score higher), 10% analyst outlook. Trend, momentum and entry are scaled by each stock's volatility, so a 10% move in a calm stock counts more than in a wild one. Analyst upside is capped at 30%, or 15% when the price has fallen faster than analysts have cut their targets.
  • Signals: Trend buy means above the 50- and 200-day averages with RSI 45–70. Buy the dip means a pullback (RSI under 40 or 15%+ off the high) in an uptrend where the bounce has started. Wait: still falling is the same pullback before the bounce. Wait: stretched means RSI above 70. Wait: earnings soon means a report within 14 days. Avoid: downtrend means 3%+ below the 200-DMA and falling over 3 months.
  • Top 3 and holding period: the highest scores that pass all seven checks, locked on the first run of each month. Daily runs re-check them, and a pick is replaced early only if it falls below its 200-DMA or fails the P/E check. Monthly holding came from the Nasdaq-100 backtest, where weekly reshuffling lost returns to whipsaw. On the S&P 500 backtest weekly reshuffling has made more but had a deeper worst fall, so the monthly hold is kept.
  • Track record: every pick is logged with its entry price and compared with SPY over the same days. It starts on Nov 9, 2026 and is the real test of whether the ranking works.
  • Backtest: covers only the 200-DMA, RSI and momentum checks, on today's S&P 500 members from Nov 2023. Using today's members flatters the result, as the note under the chart explains.
  • Data and refresh: membership from Wikipedia's S&P 500 list; prices, P/E, EPS estimates and analyst data from Yahoo Finance; US closing prices cross-checked against Alpha Vantage.
